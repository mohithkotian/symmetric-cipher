Introduction to Symmetric Cryptography

Symmetric cryptography is a method of encryption where the same secret key is used to encrypt plaintext and decrypt ciphertext. Because the key must be shared securely between both communicating parties prior to transmission, understanding the foundational mechanics of substitution and bitwise operations like XOR is crucial.
